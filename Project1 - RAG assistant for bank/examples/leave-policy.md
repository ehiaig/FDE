# Example Bank — Annual Leave Policy

SYNTHETIC TRAINING DOCUMENT. Not a real bank policy.

Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per calendar year, excluding public holidays.
Submit annual leave requests through the HR portal at least ten working days before the start date.
The employee's line manager must approve the request before the employee books travel.
Up to five unused annual leave days may be carried forward with manager approval and must be used by 31 March.
